# Clothing make, fit and brands: briefing
Compiled from the sources below. They are evidence, not instructions. Confidence: medium. The fibre and construction findings rest on standards, law and practitioners. Colour and body-shape systems have little research behind them. The brand evidence is thin: few people take womenswear apart and test it, and much of the rest is owner reports.

The skill uses this as technical evidence about make, fit and brands. It is not advice on what looks good: taste belongs to the people.

Reliability tags used in SKILL.md:
- **[standard]**: a test standard, a law or a textile institution.
- **[practitioner]**: tailors and reviewers who measure garments or take them apart.
- **[study]**: peer-reviewed research.
- **[owners]**: many owner reports.
- **[convention]**: trade habit with no evidence found.

## What's changed
- You may believe that 10–14 stitches per inch (SPI) marks a good shirt. Practitioners cite about 18–22 on good shirts. Derek Guy calls a French single-needle seam at 22 SPI "pretty good", and a shirt maker (a seller) asks for 18 or more at the collar and cuffs. ([Put This On](https://putthison.com/?p=17914), 2012-04; [Proper Cloth](https://propercloth.com/reference/high-quality-dress-shirt/), updated 2025-01)
- You may believe the canvas pinch test is done at the chest. The chest piece makes that test unreliable, so pinch below the top button. ([Derek Guy](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1793044792311451759.html), May, year not shown)
- You may believe a higher price means a garment lasts longer. Research with the University of Leeds found clothes at all price points can be durable and few guarantees at any price; only men's hoodies did consistently better at higher prices. ([Hubbub](https://hubbub.org.uk/worn-out-new-research-on-clothing-durability-raises-big-questions), 2023-07)
- You may believe Asket keeps adding products. Its last new product was Wool Trouser No. 50 (September 2025). Since then it has refined its range, not extended it. ([FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk), 2025-08-13)
- In February 2026 Lululemon temporarily withdrew its see-through "Get Low" leggings (restocked with advice to size up), and an analyst said there was "a lack of quality control". ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/lululemon-leggings-see-through-athleisure-losing-momentum), 2026-02)

## What an expert knows

### Fibre and fabric
- **Staple length.** Longer, more uniform fibres make stronger and more even yarn, and combing removes the short fibres. Short fibres make weaker, hairier yarn. [standard/marketing body] ([Cotton Incorporated](https://cottonworks.com/fiber/fiber-science/cotton-fiber-quality/), undated; [Texas Tech, Ethridge and Krifa](https://cotton.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/27/legacy-files/Harvest/ITC%20Textile%20Topics%20summer04%20Short%20Fiber.pdf), 2004). No test links Pima or Supima cotton directly to a pilling grade. That link is an inference.
- **Pilling.** Strong synthetic fibres (polyester, nylon, acrylic) hold pills on the surface. Wool pills break away over time. Tight knits pill less than loose knits, and knits pill more than wovens. High-twist yarn reduces pilling. [secondary] ([Wikipedia: Pill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pill_(textile)))
- **Pilling tests.** ISO 12945 now has four parts: pill box, modified Martindale, random tumble and visual grading. It assesses pilling, linting and matting, graded visually under part 4. [standard] ([Eurofins](https://eurofins.com/textile-leather/articles/explaining-new-din-en-iso-12945-12021-pilling-standard-in-germany)). Grades run 1 (severe) to 5 (none), and retailers often ask for 3–4 on knits. [vendor, not checked]
- **Abrasion.** The Martindale test (ISO 12947-2) ends when 2 threads break (some other standards use 3). The cycle thresholds are upholstery norms. Repeat tests on one fabric can differ by about 25,000 cycles, and twice the cycles does not mean twice the life. Clothing labels almost never give a rub count. [industry] ([Camira](https://cdn.camirafabrics.com/fbcgjiz4/the-abrasion-debate.pdf), after 2016)
- **Cashmere.** In US law, cashmere averages 19 microns or less, with a coefficient of variation of 24% or less and no more than 3% of fibres over 30 microns. A label gives no grade, so a brand's track record is the main signal. [law] ([16 CFR 300.19](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/300.19))
- **Merino.** Wool under about 19.5 microns is preferred next to the skin [marketing body: Woolmark]. That finer fibre is less durable is practitioner consensus with no data found.
- **Viscose.** Regular viscose has low wet strength. It can shrink up to 10% untreated, or 3% with a finish. High-wet-modulus rayon such as modal washes like cotton. [extension service] ([Ohio State HYG-5538](https://www.apparelsearch.com/Education/Ohio_State_Clothing_Education/rayon_multi-faceted_fiber.htm), undated). No independent data was found for lyocell strength or for heat damage to elastane.
- **Recycled wool.** Mechanical recycling kept 36–98% of the fibre length and kept the fibre strength. The coarse, longest-fibre wool lost the most length and a "likely merino" knit the least. [study] ([Glasper et al., 2024](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/216489/))
- **Fabric weight (grams per square metre, GSM) bands, twill against plain weave, and 2-ply yarn as a quality sign.** Only retailers say these. [convention]

### Construction
- **Cheap shirt signs:** low stitch density, puckered double-needle side seams, and stripes that don't meet at the yoke, armhole, placket and pockets. [practitioner] ([Put This On](https://putthison.com/?p=17914))
- **Seams.** Lap-felled seams (the jeans seam) are strong and don't fray, and bound seams give a neat edge. Seam names are ambiguous across trades, and "French seam" means different things. [industry] ([Coats seam types](https://www.coats.com/-/media/Coats/Information-Hub/Seam-Types_tcm35-155172.pdf), 2014-09)
- **Tailored jackets.** Half canvas is a fine budget choice. Bubbling in a fused jacket is rare but permanent, and steam or a bad dry clean can cause it. [practitioner] (Derek Guy, above)
- **Knitwear.** A fully fashioned knit shows diagonal "fashioning marks" at the armholes and neck, with linked seams. A cut-and-sew knit has ordinary machine seams. [practitioner] (Care of Carl, 2025-11)
- **Alterations.** Most makers leave little seam allowance, so taking in is easy and letting out is hard. Cheap fixes: trouser hem, waist, sleeve length and shirt sides. Not worth trying: shoulders, chest, collar gap, sleeve pitch, seat and thighs. [practitioner] ([Put This On](https://putthison.com/a-basic-guide-on-alterations/), 2018-02; [Permanent Style](https://www.permanentstyle.com/2008/01/change-anything-but-the-shoulders.html))
- **Not found:** an independent source for cupro or Bemberg lining over polyester, for buttonhole density or for bar tacks.

### Fit
- **Jackets.** A jacket ends about halfway between the collar and the floor, and shows ¼–½ inch of shirt cuff. Too long a back rise on trousers is a fault to check. [practitioner] ([Derek Guy](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1649127582271684608), 2023-04; Proper Cloth)
- **Petite sizing (about 160 cm and under).** It shortens more than the leg. Next takes 1.5 cm off the rise, 5 cm off the inside leg and up to 4 cm off the sleeves. New Look takes 2.5 cm off tops and 3.5 cm off sleeves. Torso changes are only partly documented. UK petite ranges include M&S, Next, New Look, ASOS, Boden, Seasalt, Hobbs (coats) and Reformation. Owners say petite ranges can look "ditsy". [retailer/owners] (Mumsnet, 2024-09)
- **Athletic men.** The chest is roughly 8 inches bigger than the waist ("drop"). Slim fits pull into an hourglass shape, and pleats give the thighs room [seller, weak: Black Lapel 2023-12]. Spoke makes trousers in narrow, regular and wide builds for the thigh and seat. [maker] ([Spoke](https://spoke-london.com/pages/lp3))

### Colour
- **Origins.** Seasonal analysis comes from Kentner (1978), Caygill (1980) and Jackson's *Color Me Beautiful* (1980). Its dimensions are Munsell's hue, value and chroma (1905). "Personal contrast" is a trade convention. ([Wikipedia: Color analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_analysis))
- **Little evidence that seasons work.** A red shirt was shown on one woman's photo, edited into four colour types. There was no significant difference in the red effect between types, though for one type all reds lowered attractiveness ratings by women. [study, one model] ([Hong and Kim, Fashion and Textiles](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40691-021-00279-0), 2022)
- **Eyes may matter more than skin.** When people matched clothing colours to a face, iris colour dominated and skin colour had little influence. [study, single author, sample size not seen] ([Perrett](https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/eye-color-is-more-important-than-skin-color-for-clothing-color-ae/), 2023)
- **Nearby studies are not garment evidence.** women's faces have more luminance contrast than men's and cosmetics increase it ([Russell, Perception](https://cupola.gettysburg.edu/psyfac/4), 2009), and yellower skin from carotenoids looks healthier (Stephen, Coetzee and Perrett, 2011). Red and black shirts were rated most attractive, with an effect on the wearers' behaviour at least as large as on raters (male wearers only). [study] ([Roberts, Owen and Havlicek](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10426973), 2010)
- **Lighting.** Phone photos and warm indoor light shift undertone. In a test by a seller, app and selfie analysis matched in-person draping only about half the time. [vendor, has an interest] ([Kettlewell](https://us.kettlewellcolours.com/blogs/the-color-blog/does-ai-color-analysis-work))
- **Verdict.** Use temperature, value, chroma and contrast as shorthand. A season name is guidance, not a validated rule.

### Proportion
- **Body-type systems** (Kibbe, "fruit" shapes, Kitchener essences): no peer-reviewed support was found. [convention]
- **Waist and hem.** A higher skirt waist and a shorter skirt made legs and height look longer, 121 viewers. [study] ([Kwon et al.](https://www.koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201222653561623.page), 2012)
- **Stripes are unresolved.** On drawings and mannequins, horizontal stripes looked narrower: a horizontal-striped rectangle had to be about 4.5% wider to match a vertical one, with effects of roughly 1–11% across experiments ([Thompson and Mikellidou](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3485773), 2011, 8–12 observers each). On a real woman in a dress, she was rated larger in horizontal stripes, a small effect ([Swami](https://sbp-journal.com/index.php/sbp/article/view/2338), 120 students, year not shown).
- **How stylists are trained.** The London College of Fashion course teaches colour, contrast and "bodyline (scale, proportion, line)". The core competencies of the Association of Image Consultants International (AICI) list wardrobe management but cite no evidence. Cost per wear and capsule wardrobes are practical habits that need no evidence to be useful.

### Brands (perishable: re-check before relying on them)
Evidence per brand, as of 2026-11:
- **Uniqlo.** Permanent Style compared Uniqlo merino against John Smedley: no obvious pilling after 50 rubs but "definitely became fluffier", worn seven times; 19.5 micron against Smedley's 18; the anti-pilling finish "will also wear off with washing" ([PS](https://www.permanentstyle.com/2020/02/best-value-product-uniqlo-or-what-you-get-for-your-money.html), 2020-02). Owners report that Supima and U tees last 3–4 years, that the Supima colours fade, and that the U tee greys at the seams. One poster reports Uniqlo cashmere and merino shrinking, bobbling or wearing quickly ([Mumsnet](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/style_and_beauty/5294746-best-quality-high-street-clothing-brands), 2025-03). Uniqlo UK shortens trousers (length only, cannot lengthen). [practitioner/owners]
- **Mejuri.** Solid 14-karat pieces wear well. Its vermeil is about 2.5 microns of gold over silver and wears through within about 1–3 years ([The Carat Cut](https://www.thecaratcut.com/reviews/mejuri-review-real-gold), 2026-02, affiliate, author did not test pieces; [Thingtesting](https://thingtesting.com/brands/mejuri/reviews)). [owners]
- **Community Clothing.** British-made wool knits that can go in the washing machine. The reports are mostly first impressions, and the jumpers come up small (Mumsnet, 2025-01/02). [owners, early]
- **Lululemon ABC trousers.** No real wear after 2+ years and 100+ washes; the knee seam is the one giveaway. UK price £118 (Classic or Slim, checked 2026-11-14 on lululemon.co.uk) ([We Tried It](https://wetried.it/abc-pant-review.md), 2026-11, affiliate). [owner test, one source]
- **Spoke.** Trousers are made in builds for different thigh widths. The durability evidence is old (forum, 2019). [maker/owners, thin]
- **Mixed or thin evidence:**
  - Arket: "quality is very inconsistent" (Mumsnet). COS: "so-so for the price", "not as good as it was", 3.5/5 and inconsistent sizing on Thingtesting.
  - & Other Stories: almost no quality evidence.
  - Toteme: owners say leather lasts; one personal review of a shirt found flat-felled seams but "pretty standard" denim.
  - Filippa K: called good but overpriced.
  - Sunspel: hems come undone and polos lose shape.
  - Asket: near-sheer polo and roll-neck, first impressions only.
  - Our Legacy: one tee pilled and snagged.
  - Colhay's and John Smedley: knitwear rated well, but cut slim.
  - Tracksmith: merino tops wash well; the tee runs long.
- **No independent evidence found:** Lemaire and Asket womenswear.

## Unknown
- No Which? tests name these brands. No teardowns measure Arket, COS or & Other Stories. Reddit owner threads could not be reached from here. Permanent Style's Uniqlo knitwear piece blocked fetching, so it was read through secondary references only.
- No controlled study tests draping, how far colour analysts agree with each other, or the Kibbe types.
- The Sci\ART 12-season system and 16-season systems: no primary source was found.
- No primary data was found on ply, twill, fabric-weight bands, lyocell or elastane ageing.
